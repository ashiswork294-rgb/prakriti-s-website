About me

A little introduction, beyond the certificates and trophies.

🌼

Curious by nature, creative by heart.

Namaste! I’m Prakriti Sapkota, a Grade 9 student at Annapurna Secondary School. I spent my school years from Nursery through Grade 8 at Everest Academy, where I discovered and developed my interests in public speaking, poetry, dance, and leadership. I enjoy expressing ideas, learning from every experience, and stepping onto a stage even when it feels a little scary. For me, every competition is a chance to learn, every poem is a new way to share a thought, and every team is an opportunity to grow. I’m still discovering what I can do, and I’m excited for all the stories that are yet to come!
